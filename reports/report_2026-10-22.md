# J2 Weekly Report: Model vs Market Disagreements

Method: Poisson model vs Pinnacle (margin removed). The market is usually right when they disagree, so I only move from the market for a concrete, post-match reason. Results below are copied from the database as given.

## 1. Oita Trinita (home) vs Ventforet Kofu (away), 25 Oct, 05:00 UTC
Model 67/20/12, Pinnacle 50/28/22 (gap 17 pts)

- **Oita form:** Their last five are D, W, W, W, D, with 0-0 at Jubilo Iwata on 21 Oct as the latest.
- **Oita news:** Defender Pereira was ruled out with a fibula fracture and ankle ligament damage, announced 18 Oct after a 10 Oct match. Forward Udo has a muscle strain (announced 11 Oct). Both pre-date the 21 Oct match, so most of this is already in the results.
- **Kofu form:** Very poor. Losses of 0-3 (Yokohama FC, 21 Oct) and 0-5 at Iwaki (18 Oct), with 1-5 and 1-2 losses before that.
- **Kofu news:** Defender Inoue has a torn ACL (announced 6 Oct, out about eight months). Forward Mitsuhira has a six-month layoff from August. Midfielder Mizuno's marriage is not relevant.
- **Assessment:** Injuries explain some caution about Oita, but not the size of the gap. Kofu's collapse favours the model more than the market. I found no news explaining why the market is so cautious.

**Verdict: Partly explained.**
**Agent forecast: 55/26/19**

## 2. Fujieda MYFC (home) vs Tokushima Vortis (away), 24 Oct, 05:00 UTC
Model 47/28/25, Pinnacle 37/31/33 (gap 10 pts)

- **Fujieda form:** D, D, L, D, D. Four draws in five and only four goals scored in that run.
- **Fujieda news:** Forward Kikui has a recurrent shoulder dislocation, out four to five months (announced 16 Oct, so it is new since the 3 Oct match but before their last two games). Midfielder Sugita is out with a re-injury (announced 4 Oct), and midfielders Miki and Nakamura were hurt in late August.
- **Tokushima form:** L, D, L, L, W. Their 21 Oct home loss to Vanraure Hachinohe is the latest result, and I found no report explaining it.
- **Tokushima news:** Midfielder Inami has a soleus strain, announced 15 Oct (a week before the 21 Oct match, so partly reflected). Midfielder Takada has a torn ACL from September.
- **Assessment:** Both sides have midfield and attacking injuries, which roughly cancel out. Fujieda's home weakness explains some of the market's lower home probability. The market's lean towards Tokushima is only partly explained by their injuries and poor recent results.

**Verdict: Partly explained.**
**Agent forecast: 41/29/30**

## 3. Montedio Yamagata (home) vs Tegevajaro Miyazaki FC (away), 25 Oct, 05:00 UTC
Model 41/28/30, Pinnacle 33/29/38 (gap 9 pts)

- **Montedio form:** L, D, L, L, W. Scoring is a problem: they lost 0-2 at home to Consadole Sapporo on 21 Oct, and only one win in their last five.
- **Montedio news:** A fan-run social media post dated after the 21 Oct match claims head coach Yokouchi has resigned. I could not confirm this from the club or a major outlet. If true, it is new information since the last match and would explain the market moving towards the away side.
- **Miyazaki form:** W, D, D, D, W, which includes a 2-1 home win over AFC Blaublitz Akita on 21 Oct. They have not lost in six J2 matches, according to one match-stats site.
- **Miyazaki news:** I found no current injury or suspension news that would change the picture.
- **Assessment:** The market's lean towards Tegevajaro Miyazaki FC is plausibly explained by the coaching change. The claim is unverified, so I only partly move towards the market.

**Verdict: Partly explained.**
**Agent forecast: 35/28/37**
