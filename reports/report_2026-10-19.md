# J2 Weekly Report: Model vs Market Disagreements

Scope: the three largest model-vs-Pinnacle gaps. Model and market figures match the stored predictions (snapshot 19 Oct 2026). None of the three gets a full "Explained" verdict; all three are partly explained.

## 1. Albirex Niigata vs Sagan Tosu (21 Oct, 10:00 UTC)
- Model H/D/A 45/26/29; Pinnacle 28/28/44. The market prices Sagan away far above the model.
- Albirex lost 1-5 at Oita on 17 Oct, ending a six-match unbeaten run. The database confirms the 5-1 score.
- A pre-match preview said midfielder Yusuke Ohnishi would miss that Oita game through suspension. I found nothing indicating this affects the Sagan match.
- Sagan's injuries: midfielder Nagi Matsumoto has a fractured fifth metatarsal, expected out about three months. Forward Daichi Suzuki is expected out about 12 weeks with a lumbar fracture.
- Sagan beat RB Omiya Ardija 2-1 on 17 Oct (database).

**Verdict: Partly explained.** The Albirex collapse gives a plausible reason for part of the swing. Sagan's injuries cut against the market's pro-Sagan price, and I found no news explaining the rest of the move.

## 2. Jubilo Iwata vs Oita Trinita (21 Oct, 10:00 UTC)
- Model H/D/A 37/28/35; Pinnacle 50/28/22. The market prices Jubilo home well above the model.
- Oita: defender Pereira suffered a fibula fracture and ligament damage on 10 Oct, and forward Shuto Udo has a rectus femoris strain. Both support a home lean.
- Oita are in form: 5-1 at Albirex (database) and a 2-1 win over Yokohama FC on 10 Oct.
- Jubilo: midfielder Tokumo Kawai (fifth metatarsal, surgery, about three months) and defender Katsunori Ueebisu (tendon injury, surgery, three to four months) are both out. This cuts against the market.
- Jubilo drew 1-1 at Tegevajaro Miyazaki on 17 Oct (database).

**Verdict: Partly explained.** Oita's injuries account for part of the home lean. Jubilo's absences and Oita's recent form pull the other way, and the 13-point gap is larger than the news supports.

## 3. Montedio Yamagata vs Consadole Sapporo (21 Oct, 04:00 UTC)
- Model H/D/A 53/26/22; Pinnacle 40/27/33. The market moves toward Consadole.
- Montedio lost 0-1 at home to Vegalta Sendai on 11 Oct and drew 0-0 at Fujieda on 17 Oct (database). A form summary shows one win in their last five.
- I found no new Montedio injury or line-up news.
- Consadole won 1-0 at Blaublitz Akita on 17 Oct, and lost 0-1 at Jubilo on 10 Oct.
- Consadole injuries: forward Haruto Shirai (calf strain, announced 17 Oct) and defender Shoya Nose (biceps injury from 24 Sep).

**Verdict: Partly explained.** Montedio's poor form plausibly drives some of the away shift, and Consadole's win supports it. The Consadole injuries are mixed, and nothing I found explains a move from 22% to 33% in full. The model's 53% home figure looks the most exposed to error.

## Caveats
- I cannot see the model's inputs. Predictions were generated on 19 Oct, after the 17 Oct results, so the model may already include the Albirex, Oita and Montedio results.
- Some club-site records are stale, so the 17 Oct results rely on the database and match-report pages.
- This is analysis, not betting advice.
